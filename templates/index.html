Design principles
⊡
Transparent assumptions
Every input, weight, and formula is visible. No black boxes. You should understand why the tool says what it says.
◎
Valuation-grounded
Returns are derived from current CAPE ratios, not naive historical averages. At CAPE 32, the next decade looks different from CAPE 15.
△
Honest about uncertainty
Outputs are probability distributions, not predictions. The tool shows you the range of outcomes, not a comfortable single number.
